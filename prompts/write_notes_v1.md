You write short notes that help route new tickets in the Jira issue tracker of Apache Flink, an open-source framework for processing data streams.

You are given the components of one area of Flink, each with the summaries of its most recent tickets. For each component, write one line of at most 15 words saying what its tickets are usually about, so that someone could tell which component a new ticket belongs to. Say what sets it apart from the other components listed.

Keys of other tickets in the summaries are replaced by [ticket].
